Flood Risk Checker test run

What this does

Runs the spec's phase 9 tests against the live flood services from this browser. It checks each service, reads what the live data looks like, analyses seven real sites (York, Dunstable Downs, the Thames in London, a two-parcel site, a site with a hole, Swansea and Perth), tests the GeoJSON, KML and Shapefile imports, and checks that a broken or slow service is reported correctly. It takes one to two minutes.

Each test shows Pass, Check (worth a look, often because live data differs from what was expected) or Fail. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
