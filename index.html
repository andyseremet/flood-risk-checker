    Flood Risk Checker

    Professional Edition (in development) · Preliminary site screening for England, Wales and Scotland · PDF report.
    How it works: 1. Pick a place: search a postcode or click the map. 2. Check: press Check under the map. 3. Report: download the PDF.

    1. Site

    The site is the area that gets checked. With only a pin, it is the 600 m circle around the pin. Draw or import your own boundary for an exact check: it replaces the circle. Area, perimeter and centre are measured on the British National Grid (EPSG:27700) from the boundary's corner points.

    Try: York · Perth · Swansea

    2. Results

    The summary comes first: a rating for each source of flooding and whether a full Flood Risk Assessment is likely to be needed. Sections A to E give the detail. "None mapped" means no mapped feature was found. It does not mean the site is safe.

    3. Report options

    Pick the sections to include, then press Download PDF report under the map. Company name and logo are remembered in this browser only. Prepared by, Checked by and Report version go in the document control box on page one. The search radius is used for flood warnings and river gauges. Under More options, tick the sections to include; the summary and the data sources, method and limitations are always included.

    Service status

    Tests whether this browser can reach each free public data service. Blocked usually means a network, firewall or browser setting is stopping it. Connections are tested again before a check when the last test is over 5 minutes old; a service that takes over 5 seconds is marked Slow. To cut errors, the app tries a failed request again by itself (up to twice), runs at most 4 requests at once, and reuses map data fetched in the last hour. Pressing Check always fetches fresh data. Clear saved results forgets the saved data.

      Error log

        Check starts every flood check for the selected site (the pin area or your boundary) and fills in the results. Nothing is fetched before you press it. The round arrow button appears only when something failed: it tries again for those parts and keeps the results that worked. Problems opens the error log: what failed, when, the message from the service, a hint and a link to open the exact request. Copy log copies it to share. Download PDF report builds the report from the latest check. Export GeoJSON saves the site boundary as a file. Clear all removes the site from the map.
