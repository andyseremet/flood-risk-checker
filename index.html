    1. Site

    The site is the area that gets checked. With only a pin, it is the 600 m circle around the pin. Draw or import your own boundary for an exact check: it replaces the circle. Area, perimeter and centre are measured on the British National Grid.

    Try: York · Perth · Swansea

    2. Results

    The summary comes first: a rating for each source of flooding and whether a full Flood Risk Assessment is likely to be needed. Sections A to E give the detail. "None mapped" means no mapped feature was found. It does not mean the site is safe.

    3. Report options

    Pick the sections to include, then press Download PDF report under the map. Company name and logo are remembered in this browser only. Prepared by, Checked by and Report version go in the document control box on page one. The search radius is used for flood warnings and river gauges.

    More options

    Sections to include. The summary and the data sources, method and limitations are always included.
